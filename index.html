Field Guide · 01

Command the Battle Grid

Choose a unit, read the glowing hexes, then commit your move.

  • Blue hexes show where your unit can move.
  • Red targets are in attack range. Damage never misses.
  • ▲Terrain matters. Forests protect from ranged fire; hills extend ranged attacks.
Field Guide · 02

Invoke Your Army

Spend mana on Unit cards and battlefield-changing Tactics.

  • 1Choose a Unit card from your hand and pay its crystal cost.
  • 2Summon on an empty controlled Keep, Fort, or linked Garrison.
  • 3Plan one turn ahead. New units enter Exhausted; Tactics resolve immediately.
Every card has its own cost.Build the right turn from the mana you saved.
