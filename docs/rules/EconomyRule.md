# EconomyRule.md

## Mana
- Base income: **3 mana per turn**.
- Each controlled Mana Well adds **+1 mana**.
- Maximum normal income: **7 mana per turn**.
- Unspent mana is lost at the end of the turn.

## Why Mana Does Not Carry Over
- Prevents passive hoarding.
- Encourages playing cards every turn.
- Makes captured Mana Wells immediately meaningful.
- Keeps turns easy to evaluate.

## Capture Timing
- A newly captured Mana Well starts producing mana on the owner's **next turn**.
- This gives the opponent one turn to react.
- Losing a Mana Well removes its bonus on the former owner's next turn.

## Anti-Snowball Rules
- Home Keep always guarantees the 3 base mana.
- Map income is capped.
- Powerful cards should cost 5–7 mana, so owning the whole map is useful but not an instant win.
- Forts improve deployment position but do not generate mana.
- Crown Points force the leading player to expose units near the center.

## Economy Goal
- Territory advantage should feel powerful.
- Losing one fight must not make the rest of the match pointless.
- More territory gives **more options**, not unstoppable exponential growth.
