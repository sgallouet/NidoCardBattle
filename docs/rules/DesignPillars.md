# DesignPillars.md

## 1. Territory Is Your Deck's Power
- Cards give possibilities.
- The map determines where and how strongly those cards can be used.
- Mana Wells increase power.
- Forts move the frontline.
- The Crown Shrine creates conflict.

## 2. Low Randomness, High Adaptation
- Random card draw creates variety.
- Combat itself is deterministic.
- Players lose because of decisions and positioning, not repeated missed attacks.

## 3. Every Turn Changes the Board
- Summon something.
- Capture something.
- Threaten something.
- Fight something.
- Avoid turns that are only movement with no consequence.

## 4. Small Armies, Important Units
- Typical army size: roughly 4–8 units.
- Every unit should matter.
- The board should never become filled with dozens of pieces.

## 5. Short Match, Fast Comeback
- Target 20–30 minutes.
- First major clash around turn 3.
- Crown Shrine forces a conclusion.
- Home Keep capture prevents endless defensive play.

## 6. One-Sentence Identity
- **“A tactical hex conquest game where the territory you capture determines how you can play the cards in your hand.”**
